Moto Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Moto Rush (package com.twodStudio.motorush), published by twodStudio, handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, your email address or any account. The game has no sign-in and no analytics of its own. The only thing the game itself sends is your career XP, for the world ranking described below.

Advertising

Moto Rush is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: banners on some menu screens, full-screen ads between levels and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), information about how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

World ranking (Top 100)

Moto Rush has a world ranking of the 100 highest career XP scores. It has no names and no accounts. When your XP is high enough to enter the top 100, the game creates a random player ID with Google Firebase Authentication. This ID is not linked to your name, your email, your Google account or your advertising ID. The game then stores your XP, the time of your last update and the XP you added that day under that ID in Google Cloud Firestore, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Other players can see the top 100 scores, but not who they belong to. Only the 100 best are kept: if you drop out of the top 100, your entry is deleted. Firebase briefly processes your IP address when the ID is created, to prevent abuse.

You can leave the ranking at any time under Settings → World Top 100. This deletes your entry and the random ID. Your XP stays on your device. You can also ask us to delete your entry through our contact form (see Contact).

Data stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, XP, upgrades, paint colours and settings) is saved on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the game. Only your career XP is ever sent, as described under World ranking.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads and the world ranking, the advertising ID for ads, and vibration for short haptic feedback on crashes, boosts and rewards (you can turn vibration off in Settings). The game itself can be played offline.

Children

Moto Rush is not directed at children under 13, and ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send us a message through our contact form.
